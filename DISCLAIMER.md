# FeedSieve 名单公示免责声明 / Roster Disclaimer

生效日期：2026-09-11 · Contact: [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/realchendahuang/feedsieve/issues)

官网「[名单公示](https://feedsieve.win/lists)」页公示 FeedSieve 社区名单（黑名单、白名单），
并提供申请入口。本声明说明公示内容的性质、边界与救济方式。

---

## 中文

### 公示内容是什么

- **黑名单**：FeedSieve 用户举报（拉黑票）与抢救（误标票）的聚合结果，加上维护者明确维护的条目。
  入榜公式公开：`拉黑票 - 误标票 ≥ 3`，一安装一票。它反映的是社区意见的聚合，**不构成 FeedSieve
  或任何一方对账号性质的事实认定或指控**。
- **白名单**：两部分——推荐白名单（维护者公开背书并附入册说明）与博主自荐（博主本人提交宣言、
  经邮箱验证与维护者确认后入册）。入册反映的是该账号被社区或维护者认可的现状，**不构成担保**。
- **社区抢救**：被社区投票验证为「误标正常」的账号。

### 边界

- 公示名单是扩展内黄框标注的判断依据之一；**拉黑动作始终由扩展用户本人点击执行**，
  FeedSieve 不代表用户或向 X 平台施加任何账号操作。
- 名单按「原样」提供，可能包含误伤；已知误伤会通过抢救票与申诉移除。
- 第三方对名单的任何使用（转载、再分发、据此采取行动）与本站无关。

### 救济

- 认为自己被误标：在[名单公示页](https://feedsieve.win/lists)提交申诉（邮箱验证），由维护者复核；
  也可在社区抢救通道为自己积累「误标」票，净票达标自动移出黑名单。
- 希望入册白名单：在名单公示页提交博主宣言申请。
- 其他争议：[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/realchendahuang/feedsieve/issues)。

## English

### What the roster is

- **Blacklist**: the aggregate of FeedSieve users' block votes minus false-positive votes, plus
  entries explicitly maintained by the maintainers. The admission formula is public:
  `block votes - false-positive votes >= 3`, one vote per installation. It reflects aggregated
  community opinion and **does not constitute a factual claim or accusation** about any account
  by FeedSieve or anyone else.
- **Whitelist**: two parts — recommended-whitelist entries (each with a maintainer endorsement and a
  public note) and self-nominated entries (submitted by the account owner as a declaration, verified
  via email and confirmed by a maintainer). Inclusion reflects current community/maintainer
  recognition and **is not a warranty**.
- **Verified (community-rescued)**: accounts the community has voted to confirm as wrongly flagged.

### Boundaries

- The published roster is one input for the yellow-frame marking inside the extension; **the block
  action is always executed by the extension user themselves**. FeedSieve never performs account
  actions on X on anyone's behalf.
- The roster is provided "as is" and may contain mistakes; known false positives are removed via
  rescue votes and appeals.
- Any use of the roster by third parties (reposting, redistribution, or acting upon it) is not
  the responsibility of this site.

### Remedies

- Believe you were wrongly flagged: submit an appeal on the
  [roster page](https://feedsieve.win/lists.html) (email-verified) for maintainer review, or
  accumulate false-positive votes through the rescue channel — reaching the net-vote threshold
  removes the entry automatically.
- Want to join the whitelist: submit a blogger declaration on the roster page.
- Other disputes: [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/realchendahuang/feedsieve/issues).
