# FeedSieve 名单公示免责声明 / Roster Disclaimer

生效日期：2026-09-11 · Contact: [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/realchendahuang/feedsieve/issues)

官网「[名单公示](https://feedsieve.win/lists)」页公示 FeedSieve 社区名单（黑名单、白名单）
与词库（官方公布的关键词规则，另设「关键词贡献」匿名提交入口，提交先挂人工审阅队列、
审阅通过后才进入词库），并提供申请入口；「[AI 号广场](https://feedsieve.win/ai)」页公示
按公开规则可以公示的 AI 号与疑似 AI 号。本声明说明公示内容的性质、边界与救济方式。

## 中文

### 与 X 的关系

FeedSieve 是独立开源项目，与 X Corp. 没有隶属、合作或背书关系。X、Twitter 及其标志是 X Corp. 的商标；本项目只在说明兼容性时使用这些名称。

### 公示内容是什么

- **黑名单**：FeedSieve 用户举报（拉黑票）与抢救（误标票）的聚合结果，加上维护者明确维护的条目。
  入榜公式公开：`拉黑票 - 误标票 ≥ 3`，一安装一票。它反映的是社区意见的聚合，**不构成 FeedSieve
  或任何一方对账号性质的事实认定或指控**。
- **白名单**：两部分——推荐白名单（维护者公开背书并附入册说明）与博主自荐（博主本人提交宣言、
  经邮箱验证与维护者确认后入册）。入册反映的是该账号被社区或维护者认可的现状，**不构成担保**。
- **社区抢救**：被社区投票验证为「误标正常」的账号。

### AI 号广场

- 广场收录两类账号：**AI 号**（Jev 判定为 AI，且被第二个独立安装印证，或社区「是 AI」票达到公开门槛）
  与**疑似 AI 号**（Jev 判定为疑似）。疑似账号页面会显式标注「疑似」，不与 AI 号混列；被社区公开
  判定为真人、或票数争议中的账号不进广场。
- 展示内容包括：handle、昵称、头像、结论与分数、回复节奏指标（中位回复间隔、快回复占比、
  长度变异系数等）、主页公开数据（粉丝数、注册时间等）、社区票数，以及该账号自己公开发布的
  回复摘要（每条最多 120 字）和原帖链接。这些内容都来自 X 上公开可见的数据，只用于帮访客判断
  「是否值得屏蔽」。
- 广场是**模型判定与社区投票的聚合，不构成 FeedSieve 或任何一方对账号性质的事实认定或指控**。
  判定按 7–30 天过期并用新证据刷新，疑似账号尤其可能误伤；回复样本随判定结论更新，重判为真人
  时删除该账号的全部样本。

### 边界

- 公示名单是扩展内黄框标注的判断依据之一；**拉黑动作始终由扩展用户本人点击执行**，
  FeedSieve 不代表用户或向 X 平台施加任何账号操作。AI 号广场同理：广场只提供复制 handle、
  打开 X 主页、安装扩展这些便利，不代任何人执行拉黑。
- 名单按「原样」提供，可能包含误伤；已知误伤会通过抢救票与申诉移除。
- 第三方对名单的任何使用（转载、再分发、据此采取行动）与本站无关。

### 救济

- 认为自己被误标：在[名单公示页](https://feedsieve.win/lists)提交申诉（邮箱验证），由维护者复核；
  也可在社区抢救通道为自己积累「误标」票，净票达标自动移出黑名单。
- 认为被 AI 号广场误收录：在该账号的 AI 味结果页点「我要申诉」，
  或直接提交[申诉](https://feedsieve.win/lists/apply?kind=appeal)（邮箱验证）。申诉进入复核队列，
  不会自动撤下公开结论；申诉成立或社区「是真人」票达到公开门槛后，账号按公开口径撤下，
  重判为真人时回复样本一并删除。
- 希望入册白名单：在名单公示页提交博主宣言申请。
- 其他争议：[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/realchendahuang/feedsieve/issues)。

## English

The [roster page](https://feedsieve.win/lists) publishes the community lists (blacklist,
whitelist) and the official keyword packs, with a keyword-contribution submission entry
(submissions enter a manual review queue and join the wordpacks only after review), plus an
application entry. The [AI account square](https://feedsieve.win/ai) publishes accounts that are
public under the public rules — AI accounts and suspected AI accounts. This statement explains
the nature, boundaries, and remedies of what is published.

### Relationship with X

FeedSieve is an independent open-source project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by X Corp. X, Twitter and their logos are trademarks of X Corp.; the names are used only to describe compatibility.

### What the roster is

- **Blacklist**: the aggregate of FeedSieve users' block votes minus false-positive votes, plus
  entries explicitly maintained by the maintainers. The admission formula is public:
  `block votes - false-positive votes >= 3`, one vote per installation. It reflects aggregated
  community opinion and **does not constitute a factual claim or accusation** about any account
  by FeedSieve or anyone else.
- **Whitelist**: two parts — recommended-whitelist entries (each with a maintainer endorsement and a
  public note) and self-nominated entries (submitted by the account owner as a declaration, verified
  via email and confirmed by a maintainer). Inclusion reflects current community/maintainer
  recognition and **is not a warranty**.
- **Verified (community-rescued)**: accounts the community has voted to confirm as wrongly flagged.

### AI account square

- The square lists two kinds of accounts: **AI accounts** (judged AI by Jev and confirmed by a second
  independent install, or with enough community "AI" votes) and **suspected AI accounts** (judged
  suspect by Jev). Suspected accounts are explicitly labeled "suspected" on their page and are not
  mixed with AI accounts; accounts the community has publicly judged human, or whose votes are
  contested, are not listed.
- It shows: handle, display name, avatar, verdict and score, reply rhythm metrics (median reply gap,
  share of fast replies, length coefficient of variation, and so on), public profile data (followers,
  join date, and so on), community vote counts, and summaries of the account's own public replies
  (at most 120 characters each) with links to the parent posts. All of it comes from data publicly
  visible on X and is provided only to help visitors decide whether an account is worth blocking.
- The square is **an aggregate of model judgments and community votes; it does not constitute a
  factual finding or accusation** about any account by FeedSieve or anyone else. Verdicts expire
  after 7–30 days and are refreshed with new evidence, and suspected accounts are especially prone
  to false positives. Reply samples follow the verdict; when an account is re-judged human, all its
  samples are deleted.

### Boundaries

- The published roster is one input for the yellow-frame marking inside the extension; **the block
  action is always executed by the extension user themselves**. FeedSieve never performs account
  actions on X on anyone's behalf. The same applies to the AI account square: it only offers
  conveniences such as copying the handle, opening the X profile and installing the extension.
- The roster is provided "as is" and may contain mistakes; known false positives are removed via
  rescue votes and appeals.
- Any use of the roster by third parties (reposting, redistribution, or acting upon it) is not
  the responsibility of this site.

### Remedies

- Believe you were wrongly flagged: submit an appeal on the
  [roster page](https://feedsieve.win/lists.html) (email-verified) for maintainer review, or
  accumulate false-positive votes through the rescue channel — reaching the net-vote threshold
  removes the entry automatically.
- Believe you were wrongly listed in the AI account square: click "This is my account, I want to
  appeal" on your account's AI result page, or submit an
  [appeal](https://feedsieve.win/lists/apply?kind=appeal) (email-verified). Appeals enter the review
  queue and do not withdraw a public verdict automatically; once the appeal is upheld or the
  community "human" votes reach the public threshold, the account is removed and, if it is re-judged
  human, its reply samples are deleted.
- Want to join the whitelist: submit a blogger declaration on the roster page.
- Other disputes: [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/realchendahuang/feedsieve/issues).
